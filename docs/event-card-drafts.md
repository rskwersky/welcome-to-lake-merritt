# Event card drafts

**Applied on 2026-11-21** (all 15, including the new title "Shelter by the Shore").
The live text is in `src/data/events.ts`; edit it there. This file is kept as a record.

Originally: Costs, effects and who backs each option stay exactly
as they are; only the words change. Mark each card **yes**, **no**, or edit the text.

## What's new on every card

1. **A sharper scenario.** A specific place at the lake, a concrete detail you can picture,
   and a reason the group can't ignore it.
2. **A voice from the lake.** One short line in italics under the scenario, from someone it
   affects (a vendor, a rower, a nurse). It gives players a person to argue for.

   ⚠️ These quotes are **invented** for the draft. The game is built from real interviews,
   so the strongest version swaps in real lines from your interviews (with permission).
   Tell me which cards you have quotes for.
3. **Optional, not drafted:** each card names one map place (the Pergola, the bird
   sanctuary…) and that spot on the board glows while the card is open, tying events to the
   map.

Titles are unchanged except where marked "optional title", because they're in Figma.

### 1. Unhoused Occupation
*Optional title:* Shelter by the Shore (the current title can read as blaming)

**Scenario:** Tents have lined the shore near the Pergola since winter. Trash is reaching
the water, and some walkers now take the long way around.
**Voice:** *“Everyone deserves to be at the lake. That includes the folks who live here.”*
(an outreach volunteer)

### 2. Parking Panic
**Scenario:** It's a sunny Saturday and every car in Oakland wants a spot on Lakeshore
Avenue. Drivers circle the block, double park and honk at the cyclists squeezing past.
**Voice:** *“I spent more time parking than picnicking.”* (a visitor from across town)

### 3. Bandage the Bandstand
**Scenario:** The Edoff Memorial Bandstand is cracked and wrapped in orange fencing. The
summer concert series is canceled until further notice.
**Voice:** *“My grandparents danced here. I want my kids to.”* (a Lakeside Park regular)

### 4. Accessible Bathrooms
**Scenario:** The lakeside restrooms lock before sunset, and the ramp is too steep for a
wheelchair. Vendors hold it all afternoon; families pack up early.
**Voice:** *“Access isn't a luxury. It's the first thing.”* (a disability advocate)

### 5. Birds Down
**Scenario:** Lake Merritt became North America's first official wildlife refuge in 1870.
Now volunteers at the bird sanctuary are finding sick ducks and herons, and nobody knows why
yet.
**Voice:** *“The birds tell you how the water is doing.”* (a Rotary Nature Center volunteer)

### 6. Power Outage
**Scenario:** The Necklace of Lights has gone dark. Evening joggers run by phone
flashlight, and the paths feel a lot less friendly after sunset.
**Voice:** *“The lights are how you know you're home.”* (an Adams Point neighbor)

### 7. Covid Spike
**Scenario:** Cases are climbing again, and the county is weighing whether to close the
lakeside lawns. For neighbors without a backyard, the lake is the only outdoor space they
have.
**Voice:** *“Closing the park doesn't keep people home. It keeps them inside.”* (a public
health nurse)

### 8. Graffiti at the Boathouse (tutorial card, also in Figma)
**Scenario:** Overnight, a burst of color appeared across the boathouse wall. Some neighbors
call it vandalism; others are already taking photos in front of it.
**Voice:** *“Who decides what counts as art?”* (a local muralist)

### 9. Dredging
**Scenario:** Decades of silt have settled on the lake bottom. On hot days the water turns
green with algae, and fish come up gasping for air.
**Voice:** *“You can smell the lake before you see it now.”* (a rower at the Boating Center)

### 10. Recycling Initiative
**Scenario:** After every sunny weekend, bins overflow along the shore and the wind does the
rest. By Monday morning, bottles bob between the geese.
**Voice:** *“It's not that people don't care. There's just nowhere to put it.”* (a weekend
vendor)

### 11. Jam Session
**Scenario:** The Saturday drum circle at the Pergola has grown into a full party with DJs
and dancing. It draws crowds from across the Bay, and some neighbors hear it with the
windows shut.
**Voice:** *“This is what the lake sounds like when it's alive.”* (a drummer)

### 12. Wild Ride
**Scenario:** Rental scooters zip along the lakeside path, weaving between strollers and
runners. Volunteers keep fishing them out of the water.
**Voice:** *“I love that they bring people here. I don't love dodging them.”* (a morning
runner)

### 13. Vendor Dispute
**Scenario:** Every weekend, dozens of vendors line Lakeshore Avenue selling food, art and
clothes. For many it's their main income. Some neighbors complain about trash and blocked
sidewalks.
**Voice:** *“We're not in the way. We're the reason people come.”* (a vendor)

### 14. Sticky Situation
**Scenario:** A developer wants to build a café on public parkland by the water, with part
of the profits going to park upkeep. The council vote is next week.
**Voice:** *“A café is nice. A café you can't afford is a fence.”* (a neighborhood
organizer)

### 15. Storm Incoming
**Scenario:** A big storm is heading for the Bay. Every storm drain around the lake empties
straight into the water, carrying whatever is in the gutter with it.
**Voice:** *“Whatever's on the street today is in the lake tomorrow.”* (a creek volunteer)
